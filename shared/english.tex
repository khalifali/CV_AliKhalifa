\section{Research profile}
Mechanical engineer and postdoctoral researcher working on predictive modelling of particulate flows. Research combines particle-resolved simulation, Euler--Lagrange modelling and machine learning to understand and model cohesive agglomerate breakage and collisions in turbulent flows. DFG Co-Principal Investigator with university lecturing and student supervision experience.
